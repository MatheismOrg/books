# Which book each cuts file belongs to

The cuts files keep the book number the book had when the cut was made.

- SA72 T8 (October 20, 2026, Gary 13:24: "I like that there was a Book 7, Sabbath of Eternity ... possibly Book 1 could be broken up into other books so that it ends up as seven"): Book 1 became three books, and the seven books stand as in SO70: Book 1, ONE Never Ending: GOD Ordered Destiny (the opening, the core, Beyond the End of Philosophy, The Territory, No-Self and One-Self, The One Math and The Calling); Book 2, One Path; Book 3, Wrestle for Truth; Book 4, Math Abstracts The Heavens; Book 5, At the Edge of the Garden (the Seder through If Three Can Be One, from Book 1); Book 6, The Empire and the Porcupine (Who Came Close, and One Snake in Two Trees through The Art of Living, from Book 1); Book 7, Sabbath of Eternity. Books 5 and 6 are provisional titles, from Gary's section names.
- Since SA72 T12 (Gary 19:38): Book 1 runs in his T510 order again (the opening through One Math God, then the Seder through If Three Can Be One, then The Calling); Book 5 is Who Came Close (Who Came Close, Beyond the End of Philosophy, The Territory, No-Self and One-Self, The One Math); Book 6 is The Empire and the Porcupine (One Snake in Two Trees through The Art of Living). The slim files are named SB1 ... SB7 and SMOMG, the full ones FB1 ... FB7 and FMOMG (Gary 15:10, T10).
- Cuts from Book 1, and from Books 5 and 6 (which came out of Book 1), are in BOOK_1_CUTS.md.
- One Path, Book 2: BOOK_2_CUTS.md. Wrestle for Truth, Book 3: BOOK_3_CUTS.md. Math Abstracts The Heavens, Book 4: BOOK_4_CUTS.md.
- Sabbath of Eternity, Book 7 (Book 5 from SO72 T2 to T7): BOOK_5_CUTS.md.
- The old Books 5 and 6 of SO70 (The Sword, the Scholar, the Supernatural & the Scapegoat; Tribe of Truth vs Consensus), dropped at 03:35 on October 20, are whole in OLD_BOOK_5_* and OLD_BOOK_6_*, with their cuts in OLD_BOOK_5_CUTS.md and OLD_BOOK_6_CUTS.md.
