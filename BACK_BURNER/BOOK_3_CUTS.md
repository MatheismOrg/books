SB3-T14-03 (Evolution's Testimony): One variant can also influence several traits. That supports a possible route by which those influences persist. More partners do not automatically mean more children, and it does not settle the whole evolutionary history.

SB3-T14-04 (Evolution's Testimony): There are documented social benefits in other animals. Researchers followed [236 male rhesus macaques over three years](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37429903/). That is evidence about these macaques, not proof that human homosexuality evolved for war or hunting.

SB3-T14-05 (Evolution's Testimony): These are different mechanisms, not one universal purpose of homosexuality. Helping relatives can also transmit shared genetic variants indirectly, but demonstrating that explanation in a particular population requires evidence.
